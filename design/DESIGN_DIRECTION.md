# Design Direction

Use a calm, focused workspace rather than a chat-only interface. The builder needs to see what has been decided, what remains uncertain, and why the next question matters.

## Visual rules

- Left rail: project, sections, and readiness.
- Main area: one interview round or work surface at a time.
- Right context panel: accepted decisions, assumptions, and detected conflicts.
- Use plain Indonesian-first product copy; technical terms get a short explanation.
- Show recommendation and trade-off before choice controls.

Avoid celebratory progress theater. Readiness must name missing evidence and the threshold it affects.
